# OIBSIP Task 1 - Retail Sales EDA

## Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset.

## Analysis Performed

- Dataset inspection
- Descriptive statistics
- Monthly and quarterly sales trends
- Customer age group analysis
- Gender-wise sales analysis
- Top-selling products
- Sales by product category
- Correlation heatmap
- Additional sales visualization

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

## Conclusion

The analysis provides useful insights into customer demographics, product performance, sales trends, and relationships between numerical variables.

### Business Recommendations

1. **Focus on high-performing product categories:** Increase inventory and promotional efforts for categories generating the highest revenue.

2. **Target key customer age groups:** Design marketing campaigns and product offers based on the age groups contributing the most sales.

3. **Optimize pricing and quantity strategies:** Since price per unit and quantity have a noticeable relationship with total sales, experiment with pricing, bundles, and quantity-based promotions to increase revenue.
